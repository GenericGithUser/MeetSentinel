What this extension sends

While a Google Meet meeting registered for your class is open, the extension measures how long it was in each state: focused, other tab, minimized, another app in front, and similar.

For each meeting it sends your teacher the start time, the end time, the time spent in each state, and the meeting code. It does not send page titles, URLs of other sites, screenshots, or the names of other apps. Nothing is recorded when no registered meeting is open.

Your teacher counts you as present when you were focused on the meeting, or taking notes in a web app, for at least the share of the meeting they set (90% by default). Time away from the meeting counts against that share. You can see your own records for each meeting on the My records tab.

Your name and email are sent once, when you join. A copy of your meeting records is kept on this computer so you can review them. You can leave the class from this page at any time. That stops all tracking and deletes your records and any unsent data from this browser. Meetings already sent stay with your teacher, linked to you, even if you leave or rejoin; ask your teacher if you want them deleted.
